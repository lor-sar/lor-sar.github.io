Lorenzo Sarnataro

Brauer Postdoctoral Fellow
University of Toronto

I am a Brauer Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Toronto. My mentors are Robert Haslhofer and Yevgeny Liokumovich.

I received my PhD at Princeton University in 2024, where I was advised by Fernando Codá Marques.

Research interests: Geometric Analysis, Minimal Surfaces, Geometric Flows.
